Quick Tips

Author format: Last, F. M. — given names auto-convert to initials as you type

Italics: Journal names + volume, and book/report/webpage titles, are italicized. Article and chapter titles are not.

Journal names: Title Case (capitalize major words) — type these as-is, don't sentence-case them

Article/chapter/book titles: Sentence case — only the first word, first word after a colon, and proper nouns are capitalized. Use the "Sentence case" button, then double-check proper nouns (people, places, named theories) — that part can't be fully automated.

DOIs/URLs: auto-normalized to https://doi.org/ format

Up to 20 authors: list all of them. 21+: first 19, then an ellipsis, then the final author (no &)

No date: use (n.d.) for year

Hanging indent: shown here for reference, but plain-text copy/paste can't carry it — reapply the hanging indent once it's in Word or Google Docs
